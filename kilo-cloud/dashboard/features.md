# Features

### Data Durability & High Availability

Your data matters. Kilo IoT ensures durability through automatic data replication and backup mechanisms. The platform is deployed across multiple availability zones using a microservices-based architecture, ensuring resilience and fault tolerance from the ground up.

***

### Unified Device & Connectivity Management

No more juggling third-party services or external platforms. With Kilo IoT, you can manage all your devices and connectivity in one unified environment.

Through **Kilo Connectivity**, you receive global IoT coverage via our own infrastructure and a broad network of trusted partners. We also provide **fully managed LPWAN servers**, including LoRaWAN network management, so you don’t have to operate your own backend.

Wherever you plan to deploy your solution, we ensure seamless and reliable communication.

Everything is centralized—streamlining operations, reducing complexity, and saving you valuable time.

***

### Customizable Dashboards

Turn your sensor data into actionable insights with fully customizable dashboards.

* Build and configure visualizations without writing code
* Share dashboards with stakeholders
* Rapidly iterate and adapt to new needs

***

### Rule Engine

Automate responses based on real-time sensor input using the built-in Rule Engine.

* Implement business logic without external tooling
* Trigger notifications, API calls, or other workflows
* Gain fine-grained control over your IoT solution’s behavior

***

### Alerting Engine

Don’t stay glued to your screen—Kilo’s flexible alerting engine lets you:

* Send notifications to responsible teams anywhere in the world
* Customize delivery channels, thresholds, and schedules
* Stay informed and responsive, 24/7

***

### User Management & Access Control

Collaborate securely across departments, vendors, or clients.

* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) is built-in
* Assign permissions based on project roles
* Ensure the right people have the right level of access at all times
