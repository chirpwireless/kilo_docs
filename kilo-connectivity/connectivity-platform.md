# Connectivity Platform

Kilo provides a unified, intuitive interface for managing SIMs, monitoring devices, and streamlining provisioning workflows. Whether you're handling a few test units or thousands of live devices, the platform adapts to your workflow and scales as your deployment grows.

**Key Features:**

* **Fully Customizable Dashboard**\
  Visualize exactly what matters to you. Track device health, data usage, connectivity status, and alerts—all in one place.
* **Real-Time Control**\
  Bulk manage SIMs or drill down to an individual unit. Activate, suspend, troubleshoot, and monitor—all in real time.
* **Zero-Touch Provisioning**\
  Automate the entire SIM lifecycle—from testing and inventory to commercial deployment—with minimal manual effort.
* **Advanced Monitoring & Alerts**\
  Set up intelligent notifications based on usage, connectivity events, or predefined rules to stay informed and proactive.
* **Centralized Management**\
  Build a single source of truth for your global IoT operations. Keep track of connected networks, SIM activities, and service usage in one place.
* **Streamlined Troubleshooting**\
  Quickly identify and resolve connectivity issues with built-in diagnostic tools—no technical overhead required.
* **Custom API Integration**\
  Use Kilo’s IoT API to integrate connectivity management into your own applications, backend systems, or service workflows.
