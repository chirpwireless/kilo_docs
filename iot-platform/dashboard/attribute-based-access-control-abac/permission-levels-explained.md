# Permission Levels Explained

#### Owner

* Full control of the organization.
* Can add, edit, and remove users.
* Access to **Organization Settings**.
* Cannot be removed by any other user.

#### Editor

* Can perform all actions except:
  * Editing or removing the Owner.
  * Accessing **Organization Settings**.
* Can invite and manage users (if granted).

#### Viewer

* Can view assigned pages.
* Cannot edit or delete anything.
