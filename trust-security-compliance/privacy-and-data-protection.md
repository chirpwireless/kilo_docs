# Privacy and data protection

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://kiloiot.io/privacy-policy/) for the published information about Kilo's processing. Your organisation also needs to decide why it collects information from devices, who may use it and what it tells the people affected.

## Make a privacy request

Contact **info [at] kiloiot.de** (replace `[at]` with `@`). Explain the service, your relationship to the account and what you need. Send only the information necessary to identify the request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before providing or changing information. Keep passwords and unnecessary identity-document copies out of your message.

## Prepare personal-data use

Discuss the applicable processing agreement and any required recipient or location restrictions before introducing personal information. Review AI applications and integrations separately: enabling another service can disclose information to that service. Check its terms, retention and model-training settings.

For moving or removing information, read [Data export and switching](data-export-deletion-and-switching.md). Camera deployments also need the checks in [Video and privacy](video-and-kilo-lens.md).
