# Shared security responsibilities

Kilo operates and protects the hosted platform. Your organisation decides who may use its account, manages its devices and local networks, and determines why information is collected and used.

| Area | Kilo's responsibility | Your organisation's responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Hosted platform | Application operation, platform access controls, configuration and service recovery arrangements | Choose account members and permissions; protect individual credentials |
| Devices and local networks | Explain the supported connection and platform configuration | Maintain equipment and local connectivity; protect device credentials |
| Integrations and AI applications | Operate Kilo's integration functions | Review the access and information shared with each connected service |
| Alerts | Operate the platform's alerting functions | Test the delivery route, name the person who will act and plan for missed delivery |
| Customer-run installations | Supply the relevant product instructions | Maintain the host, installation, local access and recovery arrangements |

Start with the [Customer security guide](customer-security-guide.md). Confirm any specific recovery, export, processing or contractual requirements for your deployment. This division of tasks does not transfer Kilo's own legal or service obligations to customers.

When someone leaves or a credential is exposed, remove the affected access and [report the problem](incident-response-and-vulnerability-reporting.md).
