# Infrastructure and providers

Kilo uses Hetzner for cloud infrastructure. Kilo remains responsible for its application, account access, configuration and recovery arrangements. Your organisation remains responsible for its devices, local networks, account membership and purposes of use.

A supplier assessment covers the supplier's stated services, locations and period. It is separate from [Kilo's assessment status](compliance-status.md).

Contact **info [at] kiloiot.de** (replace `[at]` with `@`) if your assessment requires specific hosting, processing-location or assurance information. Explain the service and requirements you are assessing so the relevant scope and handling restrictions can be established.

The providers involved depend on the service and integrations used. Obtain the applicable processing agreement and provider information for your arrangement before introducing restricted data. This overview is not a complete list of subprocessors: the companies engaged to process personal data on another organisation's behalf.

## Hosting locations and their limits

The September 2026 infrastructure checks identified Kilo cloud computing resources
at Hetzner sites in Falkenstein and Nuremberg, Germany. This identifies the inspected
computing locations. It does not establish the location of every database, backup,
support record, assistant-provider copy or onward processor.

Before relying on a location restriction, obtain confirmation for the actual service,
data categories and providers in your agreement. The hosting provider's location
alone does not establish where all access or processing takes place.

## Requests from public authorities

A request from a public authority needs an assessment of the authority, legal basis,
requested information and applicable safeguards. Kilo's handling procedure requires
that assessment before disclosure, a limited response where disclosure is lawful,
and a record of the decision. It also addresses customer notification where required
and permitted, and requests that conflict with applicable European law.

Personal information and non-personal customer information have different legal
rules. For non-personal data held in the European Union, the EU Data Act addresses
conflicting government access from other countries. A request alone does not establish
a lawful basis to disclose the data. This procedure is not a promise that Kilo can
prevent every lawful authority request or that every supplier's safeguards have been
independently assessed.
