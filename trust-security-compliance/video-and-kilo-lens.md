# Video and privacy

Before a camera deployment records real people, obtain confirmation that the proposed service is ready for that use. Explain the site, cameras, purpose, people affected, who will have access and how long the information is needed. Complete the applicable privacy and security assessment before starting.

Your organisation must establish the lawful purpose, provide the required information to affected people and control access at the site. Collect only the footage and other information needed for that purpose. Discuss proposed analytics and external processing separately.

Contact **info [at] kiloiot.de** (replace `[at]` with `@`) with the deployment description. Arrange an appropriate route if supporting material is needed; keep footage out of the initial ordinary message.

General product information does not approve a particular camera deployment. Read [Privacy and data protection](privacy-and-data-protection.md) and [Shared security responsibilities](shared-responsibility.md) alongside the product instructions.
