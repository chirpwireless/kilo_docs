# Adding a LoRaWAN Device

LoRaWAN is known for its long-range communication and excellent wall penetration, making it ideal for broad coverage in both indoor and outdoor environments.

However, to ensure optimal performance, it’s important to assess signal quality before installing any devices. This can be done using a [LoRaWAN network tester](https://shop.kiloiot.de/produkt-kategorie/typ/field-tester/). A signal strength  **-90 to -120 dBm** indicates poor signal and installing an additional LoRaWAN gateway could significantly improve connectivity.

You can learn how to add a gateway [here](adding-gateways-to-kilo-network.md).  After adding a **LoRaWAN** or **LR-FHSS** gateway, you're ready to add your first device.\
\
To add a device to your account:

1. In the left-hand panel, click on **“Devices”**.
2. Then, select the **“Add Device”** option in the upper-right corner of the page.
3. Choose **“LoRaWAN Devices”** as the device type.
4. Select the device’s **manufacturer** and **model**.
5. Enter the **DevEUI** and **AppKey**. These typically come with the device—printed on a piece of paper, the packaging, or retrievable from the device itself. For many devices, you can also connect to the device via your phone to retrieve or set this information. Refer to the device documentation or contact the manufacturer for instructions on how to retrieve or configure the AppKey.

> **Important:** Store your DevEUI and AppKey in a secure location. You'll need them if you ever remove the device from your account and want to re-add it later. While this information is stored on the device, recovering it may require technical expertise.

After adding the device, you’ll see a confirmation message:\
&#xNAN;**"Your device was added successfully."**\
