# Ideal Use Cases for LoRaWAN

LoRaWAN excels in scenarios where:

* Devices need to send small packets of data intermittently
* Power efficiency and long battery life are critical
* Connectivity must extend across large areas, including rural, remote, or underground spaces
* Cost constraints rule out cellular or wired solutions

***

### Common Applications

| **Industry**                 | **Example Applications**                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smart Cities**             | Street lighting, parking systems, air quality monitoring, waste management    |
| **Smart Buildings**          | Energy metering, environmental monitoring, occupancy detection, leak sensors  |
| **Logistics & Supply Chain** | Asset tracking, cold chain monitoring, condition-based alerts                 |
| **Agriculture**              | Soil moisture sensors, irrigation automation, livestock tracking              |
| **Industrial IoT**           | Equipment condition monitoring, predictive maintenance, remote metering       |
| **Healthcare**               | Patient movement tracking, bed and asset location, elder care alerting        |
| **Utilities & Environment**  | Smart metering (gas, water, electricity), flood sensors, environmental alerts |
