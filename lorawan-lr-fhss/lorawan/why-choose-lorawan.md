# Why Choose LoRaWAN?

LoRaWAN provides a unique value proposition for IoT deployments that need to span large areas, use minimal power, and remain cost-effective. It allows developers and operators to build networks that are:

* **Flexible and low-maintenance**
* **Efficient over long periods without frequent battery changes**
* **Easily deployable in environments that are too challenging for other wireless technologies**

Whether you’re designing a nationwide smart utility grid, outfitting a rural farm with environmental sensors, or managing urban infrastructure, LoRaWAN gives you a scalable and reliable framework to connect your devices—without the need for heavy infrastructure or recurring connectivity fees.
