# LoRa 2.4Ghz

**Kilo IoT** supports **LoRa 2.4 GHz** alongside traditional sub-GHz LoRa frequencies, enabling a broader range of IoT applications—especially in scenarios where global consistency, higher throughput, or dense wireless environments are required.

#### Why Use LoRa 2.4 GHz?

LoRa at 2.4 GHz offers several advantages that make it an attractive option for specific deployment needs:

#### Global, License-Free Operation

The 2.4 GHz ISM band is available worldwide and doesn’t require region-specific regulatory approvals. This means you can manufacture and deploy a single device model across multiple countries without modifying the hardware or going through additional certifications—greatly simplifying logistics and scaling.

#### Higher Data Throughput

Compared to sub-GHz LoRa, the 2.4 GHz band supports **higher data rates**, making it well-suited for applications where devices need to send larger payloads or communicate more frequently without compromising battery life.

#### Expanded Channel Availability

Operating at 2.4 GHz provides **more frequency channels** than traditional sub-GHz ISM bands. This helps reduce congestion in crowded radio environments and gives network designers greater flexibility when scaling deployments.

#### Better Coexistence with Other Wireless Technologies

The 2.4 GHz spectrum is already used by Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee. LoRa 2.4 GHz is engineered to **coexist with these technologies**, making it ideal for environments like industrial sites, smart buildings, or commercial campuses where multiple wireless systems operate in parallel.

#### Adequate Range for Most IoT Use Cases

While higher frequencies typically result in reduced range, LoRa 2.4 GHz can still achieve **several kilometers of line-of-sight coverage**—often reaching up to **8–10 km** under optimal conditions. It also performs well in indoor environments with good wall penetration, making it suitable for many common IoT scenarios.

#### Cost-Effective and Battery-Friendly

Modules for 2.4 GHz LoRa are **similarly priced** to their 868 MHz and 915 MHz counterparts, and remain well-suited for **low-power, battery-operated** devices.

***

### When to Use LoRa 2.4 GHz

LoRa 2.4 GHz is a strong choice for:

* **Global or cross-border deployments**, such as international asset tracking or logistics
* **High-data IoT applications**, where increased bandwidth and channel density are needed
* **Environments with many overlapping wireless technologies**, such as factories or commercial buildings
* **Low-power image or audio transmissions**, where slightly higher throughput is required while staying energy-efficient
