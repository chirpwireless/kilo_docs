# Profile Settings

Your profile information identifies you across every organization you belong to on the Kilo IoT Server. Changes made here apply globally — your name and avatar appear in audit logs, team member lists, and notification histories.

## Accessing Profile Settings

Navigate to **Settings > Profile settings** in the sidebar.

## Editing Your Profile

The profile page contains the following fields:

- **First Name** and **Last Name** — editable text inputs. These are the display names other team members see.
- **Avatar / Photo** — drag and drop an image or click to upload. Recommended size is 400 x 400 pixels. Maximum file size is 2 MB. Accepted formats: JPEG, JPG, PNG.
- **Email Address** — displayed for reference but cannot be changed directly on this page. Email changes are handled through the identity provider.

Click **Save changes** when you are done. The button remains disabled until you modify at least one field.

## Managing Your Avatar

Once an avatar is uploaded, a **Delete avatar** option appears. Clicking it prompts a confirmation: "Are you sure you want to delete your avatar?" Confirming removes the image and reverts to the default placeholder.

## Changing Your Password

Click **Change password** to be redirected to the identity provider's password management page. The password change flow is handled externally and does not occur within the Kilo IoT Server interface.

## Deleting Your Account

At the bottom of the profile page, click **Delete my account**. A confirmation dialog asks: "Are you sure you want to delete your account?" Click **Yes, delete profile** to submit the request. This sends a deletion request to the support team — you will see a confirmation: "Your request has been submitted." Account deletion is handled by the team and is not immediate.
