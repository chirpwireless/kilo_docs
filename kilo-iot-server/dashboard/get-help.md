# Get Help

If you need assistance, you can submit a support request directly from the platform.

### **How to Find It**

Look for the **Get help** button at the bottom of the sidebar, located above the language selector and theme toggle. The button displays a help icon (question mark).

### **Submitting a Support Request**

1. Click the **Get help** button
2. The Support Request form opens
3. Enter a subject for your request
4. Provide your email address
5. Describe your issue or question in the description field
6. Click **Submit**

Your request is sent to the Kilo IoT support team at support@kiloiot.io, and you will receive a response via email.
