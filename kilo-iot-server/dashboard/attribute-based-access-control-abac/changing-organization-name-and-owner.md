# Changing organization name and owner

### Managing Organization Settings

Organization Owners can update key organization-level settings, including changing the organization name and transferring ownership to another user.

***

#### Accessing Organization Settings

1. Click on your **username** in the bottom-left corner of the platform.
2. In the menu that appears, you will see a list of **organizations** you are a member of.
3. If you are the **Owner** of an organization, the label **Owner** will appear next to that organization’s name.
4. Select the organization where you are listed as **Owner**.
5. Click **Organization Settings**.

> **Note:**\
> If you are part of multiple organizations, make sure you select the one where you are the **current Owner**.\
> Only Owners can access Organization Settings.

***

#### Changing the Organization Name

Once the **Organization Settings** widget opens:

1. Locate the **Organization Name** field.
2. Enter the new name for your organization.
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply the change.

The updated name will immediately appear across the platform, including the sidebar and organization selector.

***

#### Transferring Organization Ownership

Organization ownership can be delegated to another user if responsibilities change.

**Important Requirements**

* The new Owner **must already be a member of the organization**.
* Users who are not part of the organization **will not appear** in the Owner selection dropdown.
* Ownership transfer can only be initiated by the **current Owner**.

**Steps to Transfer Ownership**

1. Open **Organization Settings**.
2. Find the **Organization Owner** dropdown field.
3. Select a user from the list of organization members.
4. Click **Save** to confirm the change.

After saving:

* The selected user becomes the **new Organization Owner**.
* The previous Owner’s role is updated automatically (typically to **Editor**).
* The new Owner gains full administrative control, including access to Organization Settings.

> **Tip:**\
> If the user you want to assign as Owner does not appear in the dropdown, first add them to the organization via the **Users** page, then return to Organization Settings to complete the transfer.

***

#### Summary

* Organization Owners can rename the organization at any time.
* Ownership can be transferred to another existing organization member.
* Only one Owner exists per organization.
* Organization Settings are accessible **only** to the current Owner.

This ensures clear responsibility, secure administration, and smooth transitions when managing organizations on the Kilo platform.
