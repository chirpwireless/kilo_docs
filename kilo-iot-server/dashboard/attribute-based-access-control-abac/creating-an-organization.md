# Creating an Organization

On the Kilo platform, an **Organization** is the foundation for managing users, devices, and permissions.\
When you **register a new account**, the platform automatically creates your **first organization**.\
This initial organization belongs to you, and you are assigned as its **Owner**.

From there, you can:

* **Add users** to your automatically created organization, giving your team or partners access to the platform.
* **Create additional organizations** as needed — for example, one for your own company and others for your clients or projects.

You can create and manage **multiple organizations** under a single account.\
Each organization operates independently with its own **Owner**, **user list**, and **access configuration**, providing complete separation between business units or client environments.

This flexibility is especially valuable for **dealers** and **system integrators**, who often manage IoT deployments for multiple customers.\
Dealers of the Kilo platform can create client organizations **under their own account**, ensuring that all connected devices are properly attributed to them.\
This structure allows dealers to **receive credit for client devices** while maintaining clear separation between each customer’s data, users, and access permissions.

***

#### How to Create an Organization

1. **Click your username** in the bottom-left corner of the Kilo dashboard.
2. From the menu, select **Organization Settings**.
3. In the **Organization Settings** window:



<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Enter the **Organization Name** (for example, “Kilo Org” or “Clients organization”).
* Enter the **email address** of the person who will be the **Owner** of this organization.
  * Only one user can be the Organization Owner.

1. Click **Save changes**.

The new organization will appear in your sidebar.\
You can switch between organizations directly from your account menu.

> **Tip:** Use multiple organizations to separate projects, clients, or business units.\
> Each organization maintains its own data, users, and permissions — ensuring clear boundaries and simplified management.

***

#### Organization Settings Overview

In the **Organization Settings** window, you can:

* View or change the **Organization Name**.
* View or update the **Organization Owner** (if you are the current Owner).
* **Transfer ownership** to another invited user.
* Click **Save changes** to confirm updates, or **Close** to exit without saving.

#### After Creating an Organization

Once your organization is created:

* You (or the assigned Owner) can **add users** to the organization.
* Owners and Editors can manage user permissions per page (**Edit**, **View**, or **No access**).
* Invited users receive an **email invitation** and must confirm before joining.
