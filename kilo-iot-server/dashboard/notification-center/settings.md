# Settings

The **Settings** tab is where you configure _how_ notifications are delivered. You can enable or disable different notification methods and define where alerts should be sent.

Kilo currently supports the following notification delivery methods:

* **E-mail**
* **SMS**

***

### Opening Notification Settings

1. In the left navigation menu, click **Notifications**.
2. In the Notification Center, select the **Settings** tab.

***

### Notification Delivery Methods

The Settings page is divided into sections based on delivery method:

* **E-mail**
* **SMS**

Each method includes:

* A toggle to enable or disable it
* A destination (email address or phone number)
* Options to edit or remove the destination
* A field to add additional recipients

***

### E-mail Notifications

The **E-mail** section controls whether notifications are sent via email.

#### Enable or Disable E-mail Notifications

* Use the toggle on the right to turn **E-mail notifications On or Off**.
* When turned **On**, Kilo will send notifications to the email address(es) listed in this section.

#### Add an Additional E-mail Address

To add another recipient:

1. Click **Add one more e-mail address**
2. Enter the new email address
3. Save

This is useful when multiple team members need to receive alerts (for example: operations, maintenance, security, or management).

#### Edit or Remove an E-mail Address

Next to each email address you will see icons to:

* **Edit** the email address
* **Remove** the email address

***

### SMS Notifications

The **SMS** section controls whether notifications are sent via text message.

#### Enable or Disable SMS Notifications

* Use the toggle on the right to turn **SMS notifications On or Off**.
* When turned **On**, Kilo will send notifications to the phone number(s) listed in this section.

#### Add an Additional Phone Number

To add another recipient:

1. Click **Add one more mobile number**
2. Enter the phone number (including country code)
3. Save

SMS is especially useful for urgent notifications such as **Critical** alerts that require immediate action.

#### Edit or Remove a Phone Number

Next to each phone number you will see icons to:

* **Edit** the phone number
* **Remove** the phone number

***

### Recommended Best Practices

To ensure your team receives important alerts:

* Enable **E-mail** for non-urgent alerts and reporting
* Enable **SMS** for high-priority notifications (e.g., Critical alerts)
* Add multiple recipients if alerts must reach multiple team members
* Keep contact information updated to avoid missed notifications
