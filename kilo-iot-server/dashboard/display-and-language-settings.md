# Display & Language Settings

At the bottom of the sidebar, you'll find controls for customizing your display preferences. The language selector appears on the left, with the theme toggle to its right.

### **Theme (Dark/Light Mode)**

Kilo IoT supports both light and dark themes to suit your working environment:

| Theme          | Best For                                                      |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Light Mode** | Well-lit environments, detailed data viewing (default)        |
| **Dark Mode**  | Low-light conditions, control rooms, NOCs, reduced eye strain |

To switch themes:

1. Click the theme toggle icon (sun/moon) at the bottom of the sidebar
2. The interface updates immediately
3. Your preference is saved automatically

### **Language**

Kilo IoT Server is available in 4 languages:

* English
* German
* French
* Spanish

To change language:

1. Click the language selector next to the theme toggle
2. Choose your preferred language
3. The interface updates immediately

Tip: Both theme and language preferences persist across sessions — you only need to set them once.
