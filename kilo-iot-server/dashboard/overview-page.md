# Overview Page

The Overview page is your main landing page after logging in. It provides a quick summary of your IoT deployment at a glance.

### What You'll See

When you open the Overview page, you will find:

#### **General Info Section**

This section displays summary cards showing the status of your deployment:

* **Devices** — Shows the total number of devices in your account
* **Gateways** — Shows the total number of gateways in your account

Each card includes:

* A count of total items
* Warning indicators if any devices or gateways need attention
* A quick-add button (+) to add new devices or gateways directly

**Warning Indicators**

The warning badge on each card shows the number of items that need attention:

* **Not connected** — Devices or gateways that are currently offline
* **Not verified** — Devices that have not been verified on the platform

Click on a card to navigate directly to the Devices or Gateways page.

### **Favorite Devices**

Below the summary cards, you'll see a scrollable list of your favorite devices. This gives you quick access to the devices you monitor most frequently.

Each favorite device shows:

* Device name
* Last seen timestamp
* Current status

### **Notifications**

The notifications panel displays your recent alerts and notifications. You can filter and review alerts without leaving the Overview page and also mark notification as read.

### Live Data

The Overview page displays live data that updates in real-time. Look for the "Live Data" indicator in the header section. You can click the refresh button to manually update all displayed data.
