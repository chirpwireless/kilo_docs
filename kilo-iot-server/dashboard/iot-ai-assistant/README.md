# IoT AI Assistant

The Kilo IoT AI Assistant is not a typical support chatbot. It is an **intelligent interface to your entire IoT deployment** that understands natural language, connects directly to your devices, analyzes your data in real-time, and provides actionable insights on demand.

Think of it as having a data analyst who knows your entire IoT infrastructure, is available 24/7, and responds in seconds.

***

## What Makes This Different

Traditional chatbots answer pre-programmed questions. The Kilo IoT Assistant does something fundamentally different:

| Traditional Chatbot         | Kilo IoT AI Assistant                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Answers FAQ-style questions | Queries your live device data                  |
| Static responses            | Real-time analysis and calculations            |
| Limited to text             | Generates charts and visualizations            |
| Generic support             | Knows YOUR devices, YOUR data, YOUR deployment |

***

## Real-World Capabilities

### Talk to Your Devices

Ask about any device in your deployment using natural language:

* "What is the current temperature in the cold storage unit?"
* "Is the warehouse humidity sensor online?"
* "Show me all devices that reported in the last hour"

### Analyze Historical Data

Get insights from your telemetry without writing queries:

* "What was the average refrigerator temperature last week?"
* "Give me the min and max humidity readings for Building A this month"
* "How many times did the freezer temperature exceed -15°C in the past 30 days?"

### Track Events and Patterns

Investigate what happened and when:

* "Show me all events from the loading dock sensors yesterday"
* "When was the last time Vehicle 7 exceeded 100 km/h?"
* "List all door open events for the warehouse after 10 PM last week"

### Create Visualizations On Demand

Turn data into charts without leaving the conversation:

* "Create a line chart of temperature readings for Sensor 5 over the past 7 days"
* "Show me a bar chart comparing daily energy usage across all meters"
* "Visualize the humidity trend for this week"

### Get Platform Help Instantly

Access the full Kilo IoT documentation through conversation:

* "How do I set up geofencing alerts?"
* "Explain how to configure a new LoRaWAN gateway"
* "What are the steps to create a custom dashboard?"

***

## How It Works

The AI Assistant connects to three powerful data sources:

1. **Your Live Device Data** — Real-time status, telemetry, and events from every device in your organization
2. **Your Historical Records** — Query days, weeks, or months of device history instantly
3. **Platform Documentation** — The complete Kilo IoT knowledge base, searchable by natural language

When you ask a question, the AI:

1. Understands your intent using advanced language models
2. Retrieves the relevant data from your deployment
3. Performs any requested calculations or analysis
4. Presents the answer in plain language, with charts if helpful

***

## Example Conversations

**Fleet Management:**

> "Which of my delivery trucks are currently moving?"
>
> The Assistant queries your GPS trackers and returns a list of vehicles currently in motion, with their current speeds and locations.

**Cold Chain Monitoring:**

> "Alert me if any freezer went above -18°C in the last 24 hours"
>
> The Assistant scans your temperature sensor history and reports any threshold violations with exact timestamps.

**Facility Monitoring:**

> "Give me a summary of power consumption for all buildings this week compared to last week"
>
> The Assistant retrieves energy meter data, calculates totals for both periods, computes the difference, and explains the trend.

**Troubleshooting:**

> "Why did the air quality sensor in Room 204 stop reporting?"
>
> The Assistant checks the device status, reviews recent events, and provides likely causes with documentation links for next steps.
