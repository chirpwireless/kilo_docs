# Is Kilo Powerful and Easy to Use?

Every IoT platform eventually has to choose between two goals. You can make it easy by limiting what people can do, or you can make nearly anything possible and accept that there is more to learn. Early in Kilo's design, we faced that choice directly.

## Why did Kilo choose power over simplicity?

We chose power. Kilo lets you define your own metrics and units, build widgets and conditions the way your project needs them, write rules from simple threshold checks up to full expressions, and reuse them across every device. You are never told that the platform cannot do something because a template did not foresee it.

That choice has a cost. A platform that can do anything has more to learn, and a newcomer faces a blank canvas.

## What changed?

Two things made the learning curve disappear without removing any of the power.

**Ready-made solutions.** A dealer who has solved a problem many times packages the complete answer. You preview it, install it with one click, and see devices, rules, alarms, and a dashboard working immediately. You learn by examining something that already works. See [Installing a Solution](installing-a-solution.md).

**The Kilo AI engine.** The AI engine in Kilo is grounded in your own data. It reads the status of your devices, and it can also help you configure the platform: add devices, create rules, and set up alarms. It works like an experienced integrator sitting next to you. You describe the outcome and it does the configuration with you. See [AI in Kilo](../ai-assistant/README.md).

## What does that mean in practice?

| If you are... | You can... |
|---|---|
| New to IoT | Install a solution, watch it run, and ask the AI engine to explain or adjust it. |
| An experienced integrator | Start from a dealer's proven baseline, then use the full rule and automation toolset to refine it. |
| A dealer or installer | Package your know-how once and roll it out to every customer. |

The result is a platform that is unusually comprehensive and, with these two additions, easy to approach. You do not trade one for the other.
