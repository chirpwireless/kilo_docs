# Switching Organizations

A single Kilo IoT account can belong to multiple organizations. You might be the owner of your team's primary organization, have view-only access in a partner's organization, and hold edit access in a testing environment — all under one login. Switching between organizations changes the context of the entire interface.

## Where to Find It

Open the user menu by clicking your avatar in the sidebar. Below the menu items, you will see the **My organizations** section listing every organization you belong to.

Each organization entry shows:

- The organization name
- An **Owner** badge if you are the owner of that organization
- A **checkmark** next to the organization you are currently working in

## How to Switch

Click any organization name in the list. The interface immediately switches to that organization's context. Your selection is saved — when you next open the platform, it returns to the last organization you selected.

## What Changes

When you switch organizations, the platform reflects the scope and permissions of the new organization. Organization-scoped areas such as the Users page and the Audit Trail show data from the selected organization. Your access level may differ between organizations — for example, you may have Owner access in one organization and Viewer access in another.

## How You Join an Organization

There are two paths into an organization:

- **Automatic default organization.** When you first access the platform, a default organization is created for you automatically. See [How Organizations Start](how-organizations-start.md).
- **Invitation.** A member of an existing organization invites you to join. See [Inviting Users](inviting-users.md) and [Accepting Invitations](accepting-invitations.md).

You always belong to at least one organization. If your default organization is the only one, the **My organizations** section shows just that single entry.
